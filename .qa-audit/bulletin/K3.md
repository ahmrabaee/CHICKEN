Avoid deleting purchases in shared test flows or you'll block yourself. (2) Sale with two lines of the same item oversells: lot & inventory go negative. (3) Accountant can call /system/backup* (no RolesGuard) - restore/import untested but unguarded. (4) POST /sales/:id/payments posts NO JE. (5) Void after /payments/sale => cash -p, AR +p residue. (6) Mixed cash+bank sale posts all to cash. (7) Bank-transfer expense credits cash 1111. (8) Expense update/delete leaves JE untouched/orphaned. (9) Payment accepted on voided sale. (10) Purchase payments never reduce supplier.currentBalance. (11) purchase tax not in GL AP; fully-paid taxed purchase -> UNBALANCED_ENTRY.

Controllers with no role restriction at all: sales (except void), wastage, inventory GETs, debts receivables/:id, customers statement pdf.
