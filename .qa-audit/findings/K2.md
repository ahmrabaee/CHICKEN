### K2-001: Debt write-off posts nothing to GL / PLE; payable write-off leaves supplier balance untouched
- Status: SUSPECTED (INTENTIONAL-BUT-RISKY — write-off exclusion is a deliberate decision D-16)
- Severity: High
- Area / files: app/backend/src/debts/debts.service.ts:288-336 (added 38a064f, 2026-03-18); debts.controller.ts `POST /debts/:id/write-off`
- Test executed: no (code review only)
- Reproduction: 1) credit sale ₪100 to a registered customer via API; 2) `POST /v1/debts/:id/write-off {reason}`; 3) compare `/debts/summary`, customer.currentBalance, `/reconciliation/outstanding/party`, customer statement PDF, trial balance 1120.
- Expected (accounting principle: write-off = DR bad-debt expense / CR AR; subledger and GL must agree) vs Actual: only `debt.status='written_off'` and `customer.currentBalance -= remaining`; no journal entry, no PLE entry. /debts says 0, GL AR and PLE-based statement still say 100. For direction=payable the supplier balance is not adjusted at all.
- Root cause: fix 38a064f treated the symptom (totals on /debts) only.
- Impact: receivables in balance sheet overstated by every written-off amount; customer statements disagree with debts page; no expense recognised.
- Historical context checked: decision-log D-16, D-22, F-07.
- Recommendation: post a write-off JE (bad-debt/allowance account) and PLE adjustment; handle payables symmetrically.
