### T1-001: Balance-sheet PDF shows Total Equity and "Total Equity & Liabilities" with the wrong sign whenever equity accounts have a balance
- Status: VERIFIED
- Severity: High (the only balance sheet the product offers is wrong as soon as owner capital exists)
- Area / files: app/backend/src/accounting/accounting.service.ts:1871-1874 (`totalEquity = Σ equity.balance + netIncomeForPeriod`, where `balance` is debit-positive), :2044-2050/:2083-2088 (PDF uses `bs.totalEquity` and `Math.abs(totalLiabilities) + totalEquity`)
- Test executed: yes — tests/T1/01-clean-slate.mjs, PDF tests/T1/pdf/01-bs.pdf
- Reproduction: baseline → setup → manual JE DR 1111 5000 / DR 1112-001 3000 / CR 3100 8000 (posted) → normal trading (purchases, sales, expenses) → GET /accounting/reports/balance-sheet/pdf?asOfDate=today.
- Expected: Total Assets 8,700.00 = Liabilities 1,000.00 + Equity (Capital 8,000.00 + net loss −300.00 = 7,700.00) → 8,700.00.
- Actual (PDF): rows correct (Capital 8000.00, Net Income −300.00), but **Total Equity −8,300.00** and **Total Equity & Liabilities −7,300.00** vs Total Assets 8,700.00. The invariant checker (GL-05) and trial balance are balanced — only the statement is wrong.
- Root cause: equity rows are negated for display (`-e.balance`) but the total sums the raw debit-positive balances and adds net income with the opposite sign convention. `Math.abs(totalLiabilities)` additionally hides a debit-balance (overpaid) AP.
- Impact: every balance sheet printed after the owner records capital (the only way to get opening cash in, since no opening-balance feature exists) shows a non-balancing statement with negative equity. Hidden on the developer seed because seed equity is 0.
- Historical context: D-27 (net income in equity) — the decision is fine, the arithmetic is not. Not covered by K3/K2.
- Recommendation: compute equity totals in natural sign (−balance) before adding net income; add an automated A = L + E assertion to the report.
