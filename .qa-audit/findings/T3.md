## New findings

### T3-001: Concurrent voids of the same sale all succeed — revenue, COGS, AR and stock reversed N times
- Status: VERIFIED
- Severity: Critical
- Area / files: app/backend/src/sales/sales.service.ts:682-711 (sale read + `isVoided` check OUTSIDE the `$transaction`), :740-756 (lot/inventory increments), :835 (void JE)
- Test executed: yes — tests/T3/lifecycle.mjs scenario F (`lifecycle.out.txt`)
- Reproduction: credit sale 10000 (2 kg item 4) to a new customer; fire 3x `POST /sales/:id/void` in parallel.
- Expected (I-06 "no double reversal", I-42 zero net effect): one 200, two 400 ALREADY_VOIDED.
- Actual: **200 | 200 | 200**; 3 `sale_void` JEs; GL delta 1120 -30000, 4110 +30000, 1131 +13200, 5100 -13200; item 4 quantity **+6000 g** (sold 2000 g) — 4 kg of phantom stock in lots/inventory; customer.currentBalance -20000.
- Root cause: check-then-act race; the tx does not re-read the sale nor use a conditional update (`where: {id, isVoided:false}`).
- Impact: a double-click on "void" (UI only disables the button while pending) or two terminals corrupts revenue, stock and AR at once; the phantom stock is then sellable.
- Historical context: K3-025 noted the outside-tx read (SUSPECTED); D-19.
- Recommendation: re-check `isVoided` inside the tx with a conditional update (updateMany where isVoided=false, assert count=1).
