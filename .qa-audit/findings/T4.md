### T4-001: AP control account never equals the supplier subledger — four different "amounts owed to suppliers"
- Status: VERIFIED
- Severity: Critical
- Area / files: purchases.service.ts:344-347, 482-545 (JE uses Σlines, debt/PLE use grandTotal); payments.service.ts:315-327 (no supplier.currentBalance decrement); accounting.service.ts:666-746
- Test executed: yes — `tests/T4/01-purchase-xval.mjs` → `01-purchase-xval.out.txt` (FINAL block)
- Reproduction: fresh dev; fund cash; (1) credit purchase supplier 1, 10 kg @ 20.00 + tax 30.00; (2b) same paid 200.00 at creation; (3) same paid 100.00 at creation; (4,5) pay purchase (1) 100.00 + 130.00 via `POST /payments/purchase`; cancel 100.00, re-pay; supplier 2: credit purchase 270.00 (after edit).
- Expected (I-30, BP04, accounting principle): GL 2110 = Σ supplier outstanding = Σ supplier.currentBalance = Σ open debts = PLE outstanding.
- Actual after the sequence:
  | View | Supplier 1 | Supplier 2 | Total |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | GL 2110 (credit balance) | 7,000 | 27,000 | **34,000** |
  | debts (open, total−paid) | 16,000 | 27,000 | **43,000** |
  | PLE outstanding | 16,000 | 27,000 | 43,000 |
  | suppliers.current_balance | **39,000** | 27,000 | **66,000** |
  - Per taxed purchase, GL AP is short by the tax (3 × 3,000 = 9,000). Purchase (1) fully settled (230.00 paid) leaves GL 2110 with a **debit** of 3,000 for that invoice (looks like a supplier prepayment that does not exist); VAT receivable 1125 is never debited, so input VAT is lost from the books (and from any VAT return).
  - supplier.current_balance only ever increases from purchases (23,000+3,000+13,000 = 39,000) — never decreased by any payment (K3-007), so the supplier list/profile shows ₪390 owed when ₪160 is owed per debts and ₪70 per GL.
- Root cause: purchase JE built from `totalInventoryValue` (lines only) while subledger uses `grandTotal`; no tax line; payment paths don't touch supplier balance.
- Impact: Balance sheet liabilities understated by all purchase VAT; after settlement AP shows phantom debit balances; supplier balance shown in UI overstated by every payment ever made. Nobody can reconcile supplier accounts.
- Historical context checked: D-21, D-22 (Debt vs PLE accepted divergence — but here Debt and PLE agree and it is GL and currentBalance that diverge), F-08 (customer side fixed, supplier side not).
- Recommendation: post tax to 1125 (or capitalise it consistently) using grandTotal for AP; decrement/increment supplier.currentBalance in payment/cancel; add AP-control reconciliation check.
