# T8 — Business-month simulation & auditor-style reconciliation

Status: DONE. Instance `t8` (port 3113, baseline profile + setup wizard via API), variant `t8b` (3114). Both stopped.
Counts: 9 findings — 8 VERIFIED (2 Critical, 4 High, 2 Medium) + 1 Info; T8-002 is also INTENTIONAL-BUT-RISKY (D-14).

## Method (read first)
- `tests/T8/t8instance.mjs` = harness launcher + `--require tests/T8/fakeclock.cjs`, which gives the backend a clock the test can set (`GET /__qaclock?set=ISO`). No app code touched. Every document is created "live" at its simulated time (Oct 1–30 2026). So saleDate, JE entryDate, paymentDate, lot receivedAt and dashboard "today" are genuine, with no back-dating artefacts (T4-007/T1-012 avoided by design).
- `tests/T8/sim.mjs` is a deterministic simulation (mulberry32 seed 20261001) that runs through the REST API only. The DB is used read-only to find the lot/debt ids that the UI shows in its pick-lists. Setup: setup wizard → cashier (accountant role) → bank account → 7 items (whole chicken buy ≈11 ₪/kg, sell 16 ₪/kg; breast, thigh, wings, backs and minced come from cutting; liver is bought) → 6 customers, 3 suppliers → opening capital by manual JE (there is no opening-balance feature).
- Each day:
  - morning purchase (farm on credit/partial, wholesaler in cash, offal supplier by bank);
  - cutting as a stock transfer (97 % yield; the 3 % processing loss is booked as wastage);
  - 20–60 POS sales (cash 70 %, card 12 %, credit 10 %, partial 4 %, scale-barcode 4 %; ~8 % with a ₪ discount);
  - voids with re-entry, collections, supplier payments;
  - expenses (rent, electricity, water, fuel, maintenance, packaging, salaries);
  - weekly wastage and a weekly cash deposit to the bank (manual JE, because there is no deposit feature).
  Once in the month: a stock-count adjustment, a purchase edit, a credit note, a VAT invoice and a write-off.
- **Shadow ledger** (object `T` in sim.mjs): an independent double-entry ledger using correct accounting. It books expenses to their category accounts, discounts and allowances as contra-revenue 4900, and VAT to 2120. It tracks FIFO lots (stock-count shrinkage is consumed FIFO) and per-customer/per-supplier balances. Self-check: shadow GL 1131 = Σ shadow lots.
- **Per-operation divergence tracking**: after EVERY API operation the script recomputes (system − truth) for 30+ metrics: cash, bank, AR (GL / customer balance / debts / PLE), AP (GL / supplier balance / debts / PLE), inventory (GL / table / lots), VAT, net income and qty per item. Any change is logged with the operation that caused it → `out-t8/divergence-log.md`. Day-end table → `out-t8/daily-recon.csv|.md`. Owner-facing PDFs/JSON → `owner-reports-t8.out.txt`.
- Runs:
  - `t8`: 30 days. Transfer unit costs are "allocated": a careful owner allocates the joint cost by relative sales value.
  - `t8b`: same seed, 10 days. Transfer unit costs are the UI pre-fill (item.defaultPurchasePrice). Same seed ⇒ identical physical operations (the same 374 sales in days 1–10); only the transfer cost differs.
- Scale of t8: 1,119 sales (6 voided, 5 re-entered), 40 purchases (1 edited), 1,212 payments, 172 customer collections, 22 supplier payments, 30 cuttings, 44 wastage records, 30 expenses (+7 blocked), 2 stock-count adjustments, 1 credit note, 1 VAT invoice, 1 write-off, 1,441 JEs.
- Known simulator artefact: the cutting volume is driven by the part with the highest need, so slow parts accumulated (324 kg backs at month end). This does not affect any reconciliation.

### T8-001: Cash on hand per system is wrong all month — bank-paid expenses come out of the cash drawer (−12,550 ₪ after 3 bills); balance sheet shows negative cash
- Status: VERIFIED
- Severity: Critical
- Area / files: expenses.service.ts legacy JE credits 1111 for any payment method (K3-011 / T4-006)
- Test executed: yes — sim run t8. See `out-t8/divergence-log.md` (first rows) and `out-t8/daily-recon.csv` (columns T_cash / S_cash_gl / T_bank / S_bank_gl).
- Reproduction: three expenses are paid by bank transfer (`POST /expenses {paymentMethod:'bank_transfer', bankAccountId}`): rent 6,000 ₪ on day 1, electricity 2,350 on day 10, one salary 4,200 on day 30.
- Expected vs actual: expected a credit to the bank GL account (accounting principle; D-15 routing). Actual: a credit to 1111 cash.
- Divergence log: `cash_gl` first appears 1 Oct 17:00 on "expense rent 6000 bank_transfer" (−6,000), then electricity (−2,350) and salaries (−4,200); `bank_gl` mirrors it (+12,550). No other operation among the 1,441 JEs touched the cash divergence.
- Month-end:
  - drawer: truth 8,520.25 vs GL −4,029.75;
  - bank: truth 98,716.10 vs GL 111,266.10.
  - GL cash was negative at day-end on 9 of 30 days (days 7, 14, 15, 16, 21, 22, 28, 29, 30); the real drawer never held less than 5,013 ₪.
  - The balance-sheet PDF prints "1111 النقد في الصندوق −4029.75".
- Business impact: the owner cannot use the system to check the cashier's drawer (every count looks like a 12,550 ₪ surplus). The bank reconciliation is off by the same amount. The error also feeds the balance guard (T8-002).
- Historical context: K3-011, T4-006, D-15 (bank methods are meant to route to the bank). Not intentional.
- Recommendation: route the expense credit by payment method and bank account, as sales and purchases already do. Add a cash-drawer count / Z report that compares counted cash with GL 1111.

## Harness limitation
Values filled by Prisma `@default(now())` come from the Rust engine clock, i.e. the real date 2026-11-25. This affects createdAt, debts.createdAt and stock_movements.movementDate; for example, the receivables/payables PDFs print "25-11-2026" as the debt date. This is not a product defect in real use, and no reconciliation above depends on these columns.
